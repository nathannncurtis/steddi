Steddi is a navigation app for daily commuters who already know their routes. It only suggests a change when the time savings actually matter.

Download for iOS Learn more ↓
01

Your route is the default

Save the routes you already drive. Steddi uses them every time instead of calculating a new one from scratch. Your preferred roads, always.

02

Reroutes earn their keep

Set a threshold — 10%, 20%, whatever feels right. Steddi won't bother you unless a faster route clears that bar. A 2-minute savings on a 40-minute drive? Not worth the detour.

03

It learns, quietly

Steddi tracks which direction you go at what time. After a few trips, it knows if you're headed to work at 7am on a Tuesday without asking.

04

No-go means no-go

Draw zones on the map where you never want to be routed. That sketchy intersection, the school zone that adds 10 minutes, the road that's been "under construction" for three years.
