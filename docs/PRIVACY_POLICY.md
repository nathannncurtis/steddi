# Steddi Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** March 2026

## The short version

Steddi does not collect your data. Everything stays on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.

## What data Steddi stores

Steddi stores your saved routes, commute locations, driving history, no-go zones, and preferences. All of this data lives locally on your device in app storage. None of it is sent to any server we operate — because we don't operate any.

## Network requests Steddi makes

Steddi does make network requests to function as a navigation app. Here's exactly what goes where:

**Mapbox** — Steddi uses Mapbox for maps, turn-by-turn navigation, traffic data, and route matching. When you navigate or record a route, GPS coordinates and route geometry are sent to Mapbox's servers to provide directions and snap GPS traces to roads. No user identity, account information, or personal data is attached to these requests. Mapbox's privacy policy applies to their processing of this data.

**Apple MapKit** — Steddi uses Apple's MKDirections and MKLocalSearch for certain routing and search features. These are standard MapKit APIs and are subject to Apple's privacy policy.

That's it. Those are the only two services Steddi communicates with.

## What we don't do

- We don't create user accounts
- We don't collect personal information
- We don't track your location history on any server
- We don't use analytics or telemetry
- We don't show ads
- We don't sell, share, or transmit your data to anyone

## Deleting your data

All data can be deleted at any time from within the app via **Settings > Delete All Data**. You can also delete the app, which removes all stored data from your device.
