# Steddi — App Store Listing

## App Name

## Subtitle

## Promotional Text
For commuters who already know the way. Steddi only reroutes you when the time savings actually matter — no more detours for two minutes.

## Description
You already know how to get to work. You don't need an app second-guessing you every morning.

Steddi is navigation built for daily commuters. Save your preferred routes, set a reroute threshold, and drive in peace. Steddi watches traffic in the background and only speaks up when an alternate route saves you real time — not two minutes through a school zone.

REROUTE WHEN IT MATTERS
Set your sensitivity and forget about it. Steddi uses a smart threshold that adapts to your remaining drive time — pickier when you're almost there, more aggressive on long hauls. You stay in control, and you stay on your route unless there's a genuinely better option.

BRING YOUR OWN ROUTE
Drive your commute once and Steddi learns it. Save preferred routes for each commute and navigate them with full turn-by-turn. No more tapping through suggested routes to find the one you actually take.

BIDIRECTIONAL COMMUTES
Set up home-to-work and work-to-home as a single commute. Steddi auto-detects which direction you're heading based on where you are. One tap to go.

NO-GO ZONES
Mark areas you want to avoid — always. Construction, that one intersection, your ex's neighborhood. Steddi respects your boundaries.

PATTERN LEARNING
The more you drive, the smarter Steddi gets. It learns which routes you prefer for different times and conditions.

BUILT FOR THE DRIVE
- Custom turn-by-turn navigation UI — clean, focused, no clutter
- Full CarPlay support
- 6 accent colors to match your style
- Dark, Light, and Dynamic themes
- Offline caching — start a route and stay on it even if signal drops

PRIVACY FIRST
All your data stays on your device. No accounts, no tracking, no analytics, no ads. Your commute is nobody's business.

Free. No ads. No subscriptions. No nonsense.
